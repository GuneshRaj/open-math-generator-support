Frequently asked questions

How do I make a worksheet?

Choose the operations and number ranges in the sidebar. The preview on the right updates straight away. Press New Problems for a fresh set.

How do I print answer keys?

In the layout options, choose whether answers are printed in place or on separate answer-key pages, then use File > Print.

How do I save a PDF or images?

Use File > Export as PDF for one PDF, or File > Save Sheets as Images to save each page as a PNG.

How do I use the dyslexia-friendly font?

Open Open Math Sheet > Settings and choose OpenDyslexic as the font. It's included with the app.

How do I change the paper size?

Use File > Page Setup. The preview follows the paper size you pick.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Open Math Sheet works offline and nothing leaves your Mac. See the privacy policy.

Open Math Sheet · Last updated 14 November 2026

Support Privacy Policy

Open Math Sheet does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • The app works entirely on your Mac and never connects to the internet.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Your worksheet settings are saved only on your Mac, in the app's own preferences.
  • Worksheets, PDFs and images are saved only where you choose, and nothing is sent anywhere.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
